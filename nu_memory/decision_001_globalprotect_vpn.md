# Decision Record: GlobalProtect standardized as the university VPN

**Decision ID:** DEC-VPN-01 · **Status:** Implemented · *(Simulated record for demo purposes)*

**The problem:** Staff and faculty needed secure access to protected NUnet resources (research systems, administrative applications, remote desktop) from off campus, across Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android, with strong authentication.

**Options considered:** (1) GlobalProtect — integrates with the university's multi-factor authentication, clients for all major platforms, centrally managed. (2) A consumer/commercial VPN service — rejected: consumer VPNs protect general browsing but cannot provide authenticated access to internal NUnet resources or meet institutional audit requirements. (3) Per-department VPN appliances — rejected: fragmented support burden and inconsistent security posture.

**People involved:** Network Services lead (proposer), Office of Information Security (security assessment), IT Service Desk leadership (support readiness), CIO sign-off.

**Where it was decided:** Network architecture review meeting (minutes and recording linked in the meeting archive).

**Final decision:** GlobalProtect adopted as the single university VPN. Portal: vpn.northeastern.edu. MFA enrollment required before first connection.

**Expected outcome:** One supported, MFA-protected remote-access path for all campuses; required for faculty/staff access to protected resources, strongly encouraged for students.

**Actual outcome:** Adopted across campuses; VPN-related Service Desk tickets shifted from "which VPN?" confusion to routine install/connect help.
