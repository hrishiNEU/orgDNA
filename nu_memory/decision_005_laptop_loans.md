# Decision Record: Extend laptop loan periods to five days

**Decision ID:** DEC-LEND-02 · **Status:** Implemented · *(Simulated record for demo purposes)*

**The problem:** Hardware lending through smart lockers and vending machines originally offered one-day laptop loans. Student feedback showed daily returns to Snell Library were burdensome and drove late fees, discouraging use of the program.

**Options considered:** (1) Keep one-day loans — rejected, feedback was consistently negative. (2) Extend to five days — selected; balances availability of the loaner pool against genuine student need. (3) Semester-long loans — rejected for the shared pool (inventory would exhaust), though longer arrangements remain possible through the Service Desk case-by-case.

**People involved:** IT Services engagement/experience leadership (proposer, citing student feedback), Snell Library operations, Service Desk.

**Final decision:** Laptop and computer-equipment loan periods extended from one day to up to five days. Items reservable up to 24 hours in advance; late fees still apply to certain items.

**Expected outcome:** Higher program usage and fewer late fees.

**Actual outcome:** Lending program expanded to additional locations (Cullinane; later Oakland and London campuses), reflecting sustained demand.
