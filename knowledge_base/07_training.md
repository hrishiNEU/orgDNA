# Wi-Fi and Network Connections (NUwave, eduroam, NUnet)

Northeastern offers several networks. Pick the right one for your situation:

## NUwave — the main campus Wi-Fi
NUwave is the secure wireless network for laptops, phones, and personal devices in residence halls, classrooms, and across campus. Connect by selecting **NUwave** and signing in with your Northeastern username and password.

## eduroam — for visitors and travel
eduroam is the secure roaming Wi-Fi network used by the international research and education community, available at 12,000+ locations worldwide including 450+ US colleges and universities. 
- **Visiting another university?** Connect to their eduroam using your Northeastern credentials.
- **To connect at Northeastern:** select **eduroam** and sign in with your username followed by @northeastern.edu (e.g., j.doe@northeastern.edu) and your password.
- Guests from other eduroam institutions can use their home credentials at Northeastern with no time limit.

## NUwave-guest — for visitors without eduroam
Provides restricted internet access (web, email, VPN apps). Guest accounts are valid on up to 10 devices for 30 days, after which re-registration is required. If you have a Northeastern account, use NUwave or eduroam instead.

## NURes-device — for smart devices in residence halls
Game consoles, smart TVs, streaming sticks, and smart-home devices usually can't join NUwave. Register them on the **NURes-device** network using the device self-registration system.

## NUnet — wired network
NUnet is the high-bandwidth wired network in academic, administrative, and research buildings on all campuses. Plug an ethernet cable into an active port to connect.

Wi-Fi trouble? Forget the network, reconnect, and re-enter credentials. Still stuck? Call 617-373-4357 (24/7) or check https://northeastern.statuspage.io/ for outages.
