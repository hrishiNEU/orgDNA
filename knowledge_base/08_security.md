# GlobalProtect VPN: Secure Access from Off Campus

Northeastern uses the **GlobalProtect** VPN to provide safe, encrypted access to protected NUnet resources when you're off campus — from home, a coffee shop, while traveling, or on any public network.

## Who needs it
- **Faculty and staff:** required to use GlobalProtect when accessing protected university resources off campus
- **Students:** strongly encouraged; some services (restricted research systems, Remote Desktop/VNC to on-campus computers, certain library and departmental resources) REQUIRE the VPN when connecting from off campus

## How to set it up
1. Enroll in multi-factor authentication (MFA) first — the VPN requires it
2. Go to **https://vpn.northeastern.edu** and sign in with your Northeastern credentials
3. Download and install the GlobalProtect client for your platform (Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android)
4. Open GlobalProtect, connect, and authenticate with your Northeastern account + MFA

## Good to know
- While on campus, NUwave/NUnet already provide secure access — the VPN is for off-campus connections
- Connections to external cloud platforms like Microsoft 365 and Google travel outside the VPN tunnel; the VPN protects traffic to sensitive university systems (research data, student records, financial systems)
- If the VPN disconnects, just reconnect in the GlobalProtect app and re-authenticate with MFA
