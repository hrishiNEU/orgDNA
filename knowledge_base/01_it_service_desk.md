# IT Service Desk and Getting Help

Northeastern's IT Service Desk is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year — including holidays and semester breaks.

## Ways to get help
- **Phone:** 617-373-HELP (617-373-4357)
- **Email:** help@northeastern.edu
- **Chat / tickets:** Tech Service Portal at https://service.northeastern.edu/tech
- **In person (Boston):** Visit the Tech Bar on the second floor of Snell Library for walk-up help with your laptop, Canvas, Wi-Fi, and other issues. IT Services concierge staff are also stationed in Snell Library near the hardware locker and vending locations.
- **London campus:** Reach the local IT team in 1 Portsoken (5th floor, IT Hub), at First Point in Devon House, or by emailing it@nulondon.ac.uk.

## What the Service Desk can help with
Password resets, account lockouts, Wi-Fi and VPN problems, email issues, Canvas access, software installation, borrowing hardware, classroom technology issues, and routing you to specialist teams (Academic Technologies, Information Security, Network Services) when needed.

If you are not sure which team to contact, always start with the Service Desk — they will route your request to the right group and you can track progress through the Tech Service Portal.
