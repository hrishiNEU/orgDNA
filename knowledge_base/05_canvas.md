# Canvas, Classroom Technology, and Academic Technologies

## Canvas
Canvas is the digital home base for every Northeastern course: https://canvas.northeastern.edu/

- All course materials, assignments, quizzes, grades, and announcements live in Canvas
- Accessible anytime, on any device — the Canvas mobile app keeps you connected on the go
- Integrated tools inside Canvas include **Panopto** (lecture capture and video — Zoom class recordings are often published to Panopto), **Microsoft Teams meetings**, and **Gradescope** for assignment grading
- If Canvas seems down, check https://northeastern.statuspage.io/ before troubleshooting

## Classroom technology
Northeastern classrooms are equipped for flexible, hybrid teaching — engaging students in the room and across the world, with support for online content, flipped-classroom techniques, and digital media. See https://nuflex.northeastern.edu/classroom-technology/

## Academic Technologies support
The Academic Technologies team (https://www.academictechnologies.northeastern.edu/) offers consultations and training for instructors: getting started with Canvas and its integrated tools, customizing the online course environment, lecture capture, and designing engaging digital learning experiences. Students benefit from digital labs and personalized classroom solutions.

For any Canvas login or access problem, start at the Tech Service Portal or call 617-373-4357 (24/7).
