# Microsoft 365, Teams, and Free Software for Students

## Microsoft Teams
Teams is Northeastern's primary collaboration platform: chat, file sharing, audio/video meetings, calls, and real-time project management in one place. Use it for group projects, office hours, and student org coordination. ITS offers training and custom workshops on Teams and meeting tools via https://training.its.northeastern.edu/

## Microsoft 365 (free with your Northeastern account)
Every student, faculty, and staff member gets the cloud-based Microsoft 365 suite: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, OneDrive cloud storage, Power BI, Excel Online, and more. Sign in at office.com with your Northeastern email and password, or install the desktop and mobile apps.

## Other software available to students
- **Adobe Creative Cloud** — available to Northeastern students for design, photo, and video work
- **LinkedIn Learning** — free on-demand courses on software, business, and creative skills
- **Virtual desktop** — access a Windows desktop preloaded with Office 365, Adobe products, MATLAB, Stata, and more from any device by signing in with your Northeastern account (search "virtual desktop" in the Tech Service Portal for the current link)

## Email
Student email runs on Outlook / Exchange Online as part of Microsoft 365. Set it up on your phone with the Outlook mobile app and sign in with your Northeastern credentials. Northeastern will NEVER ask you to confirm your username and password by email — treat any such message as phishing.
