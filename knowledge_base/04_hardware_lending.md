# Borrowing Hardware: Laptops, Cameras, Chargers, and More

Northeastern's technology lending program lets students, faculty, and staff borrow equipment through smart vending machines and smart lockers. The service is available on the Boston, Oakland, and London campuses. Details: https://its.northeastern.edu/hardware

## What you can borrow
- **Lockers (short-term loans):** laptops, cameras, and other larger equipment, picked up and returned contactless via smart lockers
- **Vending machines (small items):** power adapters, chargers, Bluetooth presenters, HDMI cables, voice recorders, and other small tech to get you through the day

## Loan rules
- Items can be **reserved up to 24 hours in advance**, year-round
- Laptops and other computer equipment can be kept for **up to five days**
- Late fees apply to certain items, so return on time
- The pickup/return process is the same whether the item came from a locker or a vending machine
- Loaner laptops can also be arranged by visiting the Tech Bar / IT Service Desk, which reserves the laptop for pickup from a designated locker

## Locations
- **Boston — Snell Library:** Blue, Green, and Orange stations at The Hub, near the front entrance (IT Services concierge staff are nearby if you need help)
- **Boston — Cullinane Hall:** Red station (lockers only), first-floor Student Collaboration Space
- **Oakland — Rothwell:** to the left of Café Suzie's, outside the Student Center entrance
- **London — 1 Portsoken:** 5th floor, near the IT Hub Room, across from classroom 516

This is the fastest way to get a temporary laptop if yours breaks mid-semester, or to grab a camera or presenter for a class project. The library's Recording Studios also loan some specialized recording equipment.
