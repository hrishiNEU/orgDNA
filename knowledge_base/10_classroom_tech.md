# Your Northeastern Account, Student Hub, and Training Resources

## Claiming and managing your account
Your Northeastern account opens the door to everything: the Student Hub, Outlook email, Canvas, Wi-Fi, and more.
- **New students:** claim your account by clicking the unique link in the email sent to you — the link is valid for **60 days**. If more than 60 days have passed, contact the IT Service Desk (617-373-4357) to get a new one.
- **Manage your account:** password changes, recovery options, and settings at https://accounts.northeastern.edu/
- **Locked out?** Start at accounts.northeastern.edu or call the 24/7 Service Desk.
- Access to systems is permission-based: your role (student, faculty, staff) determines which applications and data you can use, protected by multi-factor authentication.

## Student Hub
The Student Hub is the personalized portal for registration, grades, billing, co-op resources, campus life, and university services — sign in with your Northeastern account.

## ITS Training
Everyone at Northeastern can build tech skills through the ITS Training Site: https://training.its.northeastern.edu/
- Live walk-throughs and in-person sessions with technology professionals
- Virtual workshops you can register for
- Step-by-step self-service guides
- Custom workshops tailored to a team's needs
Featured topics include Meeting Solutions (Teams), Collaboration tools, and Classroom Technology.

## Communities
The Tech Talk community on Communities at Northeastern shares tech updates 24/7 — a good way to stay current on new services and changes.
