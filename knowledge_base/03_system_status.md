# System Status and Outage Notifications

To check whether a Northeastern system is down — Canvas, email/Outlook, NUwave Wi-Fi, the VPN, the Student Hub, or anything else — visit the official IT Statuspage: https://northeastern.statuspage.io/

## What the Statuspage shows
- Real-time operational status for university systems and services
- Active incidents with updates as ITS investigates and resolves them
- Scheduled maintenance windows, so you know about planned downtime in advance (important around exams and registration periods)

## Subscribe to notifications
You can subscribe on the Statuspage to receive automatic notifications by email or text message whenever an outage is reported, updated, or resolved. This is the fastest way to know whether "Canvas is down for everyone or just me."

## If something seems down but isn't listed
1. Check the Statuspage first.
2. If your issue isn't listed, it may be local to your account or device — report it through the Tech Service Portal (https://service.northeastern.edu/tech) or call the 24/7 Service Desk at 617-373-4357.
3. During a confirmed outage, you generally do not need to open a ticket; ITS posts updates on the Statuspage until service is restored.
