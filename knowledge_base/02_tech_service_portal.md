# Tech Service Portal and Now Mobile App

The Tech Service Portal (https://service.northeastern.edu/tech) is the single front door for all technology support at Northeastern. Sign in with your Northeastern username and password from any device.

## What you can do in the portal
- Submit service requests: software installs, account changes, access requests, hardware repairs
- Report incidents and outages
- Browse a searchable knowledge base of hundreds of self-help how-to articles
- Track the status of your open and past support tickets — you receive a notification when your request has been solved
- Start a live chat with the Service Desk
- Manage loaned devices from the hardware lending program

## Now Mobile app
The free ServiceNow "Now Mobile" app (Apple App Store and Google Play) lets you report IT issues, request technology services, and manage loaned devices from your phone. The ServiceNow portal is also used for things like registering for EXP Makerspace credential trainings, which are required before using Makerspace equipment such as 3D printers and laser cutters.

## Tip
Before opening a ticket, search the portal's knowledge base — common tasks like connecting to Wi-Fi, setting up email on your phone, and installing software usually have step-by-step articles that solve the problem in minutes.
